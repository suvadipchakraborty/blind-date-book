About this bookshop

Blind Date with a Book is a small act of rebellion against the algorithm. No star ratings, no "readers also bought," no cover art doing the deciding for you. Just the feeling of pulling a wrapped paperback off a table at your favourite independent bookstore — mood written on a sticky note, title hidden until you're home.

Every match is pulled live from the Open Library catalogue. Some dates are perfect. Some are a little strange. That's the whole point — the joy of not knowing is the joy of reading.
